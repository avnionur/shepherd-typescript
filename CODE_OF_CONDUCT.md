# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Contributors and maintainers must:

- communicate directly and respectfully;
- critique technical work rather than people;
- provide evidence for compatibility, security, and performance claims;
- respect privacy, credentials, embargoed vulnerabilities, and attribution;
- accept correction and document changed decisions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and knowingly misleading
technical claims are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-managed
community spaces.

Report conduct concerns through
[GitHub's private abuse-report channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse). To reach
the project maintainer directly, open a
[contact-request issue](https://github.com/avnionur/shepherd-typescript/issues/new) without incident
details and request a private channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions,
restrict participation, or ban contributors when needed to protect the project and its
participants.
